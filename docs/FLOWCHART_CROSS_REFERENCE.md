## Experiment E-A — seeing versus entering (row 1)

Run with `tools/experiments/e_a_seeing_vs_entering.c` (existing chess.c functions only: `piece_attacks`, `legal_destination_map`, `load_fen`); full output in `tools/experiments/e_a_output.txt`.

- **Statement tested against** (Draft 12 §[2], owner-accepted): "Seeing the rock is a dimensional perspective and remains strictly separate from removing the rock."
- **Positions:** E5 (protection is not mobility), E3 after 3.Qh5 (the f7 pawn pinned), the start, a pinned knight, a castling position; 14 pieces.
- **Observed:** *A* − *L* was non-empty for 12 pieces and *L* − *A* for 4, with three kinds of reason for *A* − *L*: OWN-PIECE (a protected square cannot be entered: the rook a1 and b1 in E5, the queen d1 at the start), PAWN-DIAGONAL-EMPTY (a pawn watches diagonally but enters a diagonal square only by capturing), KING-SAFETY (the pinned knight on e2 watches six squares and may enter none); and two kinds for *L* − *A*: PAWN-FORWARD and CASTLING.
- **The pinned f7 pawn has two separate reasons** (clarified at ChatGPT review 35's request): its diagonal squares e6 and g6 are watched but are not legal destinations because they are empty and a pawn enters a diagonal square only by capturing (PAWN-DIAGONAL-EMPTY); its forward squares f6 and f5 are neither watched nor legal, and it cannot move forward because the queen on h5 pins it to the king on e8 (moving would expose the king: the R report's `ZERO Black P f7 PIN after`). The two reasons are independent: without the pin the pawn could move forward, and its diagonals would still not be legal destinations.
- **Result: SUPPORTS** the narrow mapping that the chess program measures what a piece watches and where it may go separately, and that the two differ in both directions.
- **Limit:** this does not show that the rock is a chess obstacle, or that seeing and removing the rock are modelled.

## Experiment E-B — a tempting capture and its legal consequences (row 12)

Run with `tools/experiments/e_b_tempting_capture.c`, which only calls the existing reports (`R`, `replies`, `exchange`, `loop`); full output in `tools/experiments/e_b_output.txt`. Approved in ChatGPT review 35. No warning-delivery, cancellation or decision mechanism is introduced.

- **Position (White to move):** `6k1/8/4p3/3r4/4P3/2N5/8/3Q2K1 w - - 0 1` — a black rook on d5 protected by the pawn on e6; White has Qd1, Nc3, Pe4 that can each take it.
- **Candidate:** Qxd5 (d1d5).

**Observations** (copied from the output; pieces are named by their square before the move, so `Q d1` is the queen now on d5):

1. *Legal opposing replies:* six — five king moves and `e6d5` (the pawn takes the queen). None is labelled STOPS or ALLOWS: White has no mate-in-one threat before or after (`THREAT White none`, `SUMMARY REPLIES 6 ... NO-THREAT 6`).
2. *Attack and protection changes (R report):* `ATTACK+ Black P e6 Q d1` (the e6 pawn now attacks the queen), `PROTECT+ White P e4 Q d1` (the e4 pawn protects the queen), `PROTECT− Black P e6 R d5` (the rook it protected is gone), `ATTACK+ White Q d1 P e6`; White's e4 pawn and c3 knight lose their capture on d5 (`LOST White P e4 d5`, `LOST White N c3 d5`).
3. *Exchange and material (limit 4):* Qxd5 is `MATERIAL POSSIBLE NONNEGATIVE` — the line `d1d5 e6d5` ends White 5, Black 9, and after a recapture White 6, Black 9; exd5 and Nxd5 on the same rook are `MATERIAL FORCED NONNEGATIVE` (White 6 / Black 1 and 6 / 3 at most). After Qxd5, Black's `e6d5` is `MATERIAL FORCED NONNEGATIVE` for Black (Black 9, White at most 1).
4. *Bounded checkmate consequences:* none. `loop 3` from the position finds no mate for either side within 3 plies (`RESULT White NOT-FOUND`, `LIMIT 3`); `loop 2` after Qxd5 likewise (`LIMIT 2`). Beyond those limits nothing is claimed.

**Candidate warning facts** (observations that could be read as a "warning"; this is interpretation, kept apart from the observations above): (a) the new attack link `ATTACK+ Black P e6 Q d1` — a piece of value 1 attacks the capturing piece of value 9; (b) the reply `e6d5` exists among the six legal replies; (c) the exchange class POSSIBLE (not FORCED) for Qxd5 while the other two captures of the same rook are FORCED; (d) the line totals White 5 / Black 9.

- **Result:** these facts are **candidate evidence** that the immediate value of a capture (the rook, 5) does not by itself establish a favorable material outcome, which is what the owner's idea 8 also says.
- **Limit:** they do not establish the flowchart's warning delivery, memory, cancellation or arbitration, nor what an "opportunity" is (that stays the owner's question). No dependency is closed.
