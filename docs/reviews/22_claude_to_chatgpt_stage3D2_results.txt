What 3D-2 adds (spec v1.8, Section 7, "3D-2 in detail")
1. "routes real N" (N = 1..6 plies): per goal square and condition (REACH,
   PROTECTED), per first move m of S:
     CONDITION IF FORCED [MOVES 1] - m satisfies the condition, or N >= 3 and
       after EVERY reply it is forced within N - 2 (existential S choices,
       universal replies, a different continuation allowed per reply);
     CONDITION IF POSSIBLE k OF r - not forced; after k of r replies the
       condition can still be satisfied within the depth;
     CONDITION ELSE.
   Then RESULT, SQUARE, FIRST-IF <sq> FORCED|POSSIBLE (the owner's if /
   else if order), LIMIT N when something is not forced. The condition is
   tested right after each S move; a line ends at checkmate or stalemate.
   Header "... DEPTH N REAL" and a NOTE; PASS reports keep "PASS".
2. "support" after a preview (PASS): every move of another S piece that
   creates a NEW protection link to the moved piece, with the protector
   count after it (a follow-up may block another protector's line; the
   count shows that, e.g. after Nf3, Be2 protects f3 but blocks Qd1: 2).
3. "goal2 <squares> <L>": with a first goal, "routes" adds SECOND ... AFTER
   <first IF square>, FROM START (the current position) and FROM <route>
   for every non-dominated route to that square (each once), SIDE REACH and
   PROTECTED for each G2 square. PASS only. First goal not reached: from the
   start only.
No weights; no move is chosen; the board, turn and history are unchanged.
