What the specification does
- Starts from your CHESS9_NEW_CALCULATOR_PLAN.txt and makes Sections 3-7
  exact; Section 9 answers your four questions.
- Keeps L (legal destinations) and A (controlled squares, including
  friendly-occupied ones, ignoring pins) separate; a protection link never
  points at a king.
- Your three counting units become U1 (zero-move pieces), U2 (lost
  (piece, square) pairs) and U3 (squares denied to the team), never added
  together, with the rule that a loss is charged once, at its transition, and
  a reply can restore it.
- Routes are compared as a vector (own moves, protectors, attackers,
  material at risk), with no weights; "coming up protectively" is a support
  follow-up: a later move after which another friendly piece protects the
  moved one.
- Stages 3A-3E as in your plan; 3A is a machine-readable report with fixed
  keywords and one square order.
- Every worked example (Section 8) was computed by tools/spec_examples/
  examples.c, which includes chess.c unchanged. Main facts: 3.Qh5 pins f7
  (Black loses f7-f6, f7-f5, h7-h5), threatens exactly one mate (Qxf7#);
  Black has 28 replies, 8 stop every mate-in-one (Qe7, Qf6, Qg5, Qh4, Ke7,
  Nh6, d5, g6) and 20 leave one; Bc4 reaches f7 in 1 move unprotected, the
  queen in 2 moves arriving protected.
