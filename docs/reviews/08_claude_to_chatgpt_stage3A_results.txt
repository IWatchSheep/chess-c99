Your eight requirements
1. Original behaviour preserved: all seven version 5 golden transcripts are
   reproduced byte for byte; R is the only new command and is never typed in
   them. The preview, P, N, F, promotion and draw flows are unchanged.
2. U1, U2, U3: ZERO lines (with BLOCKED/CHECK/PIN and before/after/both),
   LOST lines (U2 pairs), DENIED lines (U3, with by-capture); the reverse as
   GAINED/RESTORED and OPENED; COUNT per side.
3. Relationships: PROTECT+/- and ATTACK+/- (formed/broken, by identity),
   CHECK, and MATE1 (each side's mate-in-one moves after the move).
4. Identities: every piece gets a permanent number at the start of tracking
   (start, restart, fen) and keeps it across every finalized move through
   follow_piece (castling rook, capture, en passant, promotion). In the
   report a piece is named by its type and square BEFORE the move, as v1.2
   Section 7 says (readable: the pawn now on e5 is "P e5").
5. Count-once: on F, every pair the move removes is remembered as lost; a
   later move that brings it back reports RESTORED (not GAINED); a pair that
   stays missing is never charged again; lost again later = a new LOST.
6. R prints the report (CALC BEGIN ... CALC END), deterministic, one fact per
   line, fixed keywords and order; R changes nothing (tested).
7. tests/calc_tests.c (27 checks, CTest "calc_tests"): the whole 1.e4 report
   line by line; 3.Qh5 (ZERO Black P f7 PIN after; LOST Black P f7 f6 f5; LOST
   Black P h7 h5; MATE1 White h5f7; counts); 3...g6 after 3.Qh5 was played
   (RESTORED Black P f7 f6 f5, GAINED 3 RESTORED 2, the g-pawn attacks the
   queen, the threat gone) and the same position from a FEN (no history:
   GAINED 5); 3.Nf3 (LOST 8, GAINED 8, the knight attacks e5); E5 protection
   vs mobility; capture with DENIED ... by-capture; en passant; castling
   (CASTLE); promotion with capture (PROMOTED, CAPTURED); each zero-move
   cause; the lost / restored / lost-again sequence (rook a1 vs a bishop
   blocking the a-file, five moves, with checks that the still-missing pairs
   are not charged again in between); invariants (board, turn, history and
   tracker unchanged by R; deterministic; framing lines).
   The 1.e4, 3.Qh5, 3...g6 and 3.Nf3 numbers equal those computed by the
   independent harness tools/spec_examples/examples.c.
8. No strategy selection, no weights, no Side A/B.
