What 3C adds (spec v1.6, Section 7, "3C in detail")
1. "loop N" (N = 1..6, the user's choice; no default, no pruning) visits
   every legal sequence of 1 to N plies from the current position and prints:
     LOOP BEGIN <side to move S> DEPTH N [ZERO K]
     MATE <ply> <moves...> MATED <side>     every sequence ending in mate
     PLY <p> POSITIONS <n> MATED White <n> Black <n> STALEMATES <n> [ZERO <n>]
     FIRST <m> FORCED | POSSIBLE <k> OF <r> | NOT-FOUND  MATED White <n> Black <n>
     RESULT <S> FORCED | POSSIBLE | NOT-FOUND
     LIMIT N                                when RESULT is not FORCED
     LOOP END
   (NO MOVES checkmate|stalemate when S has no move.)
2. Forced and possible are exactly spec 5b, for a mate of the other side:
   FORCED within N: m mates, or N >= 3 and for every reply a mate is forced
   within N - 2 (recursively, a different continuation allowed after each
   reply). POSSIBLE k OF r: m does not force mate, but after k of its r
   replies a mate of the other side is reachable within N plies in total.
   Counting mates is never used as proof of forcing.
3. "loop N K" also counts, per ply, the sequences after which the side to
   move has at least K pieces with no legal move.
4. "trace <moves>" prints the R report of each move of one sequence, with
   the piece memory carried as if each move were finalized (charge once,
   RESTORED). This is how the Section 5 measurements are read at each node
   of a chosen line; the loop itself does not print them for every node
   (from the start, 5 plies is 4,865,609 positions).
5. Nothing is played, chosen or scored; the game, the turn, the history and
   the memory are unchanged (tested). Only checkmate and stalemate end a
   sequence; draw claims (threefold, fifty moves, insufficient material) are
   not applied inside the loop. Fixed order: moves sorted as in 3B.

Evidence (tests/loop_tests.c, 28 checks, CTest "loop_tests")
- Positions per ply equal published perft: start 20, 400, 8902, 197281
  (and 4,865,609 at 5 plies, checked by hand run, 26 s); Kiwipete 48, 2039,
  97862 (castling, en passant, promotion).
- E6: from the start, exactly the 8 Fool's Mates at 4 plies (all listed),
  347 mates at 5 plies (hand run); after 1.e4 e5, 2,961 at 5 plies (E8,
  hand run, about 2.5 minutes).
- E8: K+Q v K not found in 1 ply, FORCED in 3; K+R v K: Ra8 FORCED in 1;
  Scholar corner (Ke1 Qd1 Bc4 v Ke8 Pf7): POSSIBLE, none forced in 5 plies.
- Cross-check with 3B: from the E3 position, for every one of the 33 first
  moves, "POSSIBLE k OF r" equals the reply view's LEAVES + ALLOWS out of
  REPLIES (3.Qh5: 20 of 28; 3.Qf3: 21 of 31).
- Forced vs possible: 3.Qh5 has 22 mating lines within 3 plies but is
  POSSIBLE, not FORCED; for K+Q v K, FORCED is checked against an
  independent "every reply allows mate in one" test for every first move.
- Stalemate counted (K+Q v K, Qg6), NO MOVES checkmate / stalemate, ZERO K.
- trace: every step equals R in a game where the earlier moves were
  finalized; 3...g6 shows RESTORED Black P f7 f6 f5; Scholar's Mate ends
  with CHECK and no MATE1 (version 9); bad or illegal sequences refused.
- Invariants, determinism, framing; bad loop arguments refused.
- tests/golden_v10: 12_loop and 13_loop_boards (sanitizer build).
- CTest: 17 tests, all pass (unit 63, report 31, replies 22, loop 28, 13
  transcripts). All eleven earlier transcripts unchanged. Strict C99
