What 3E adds (spec v1.9, Section 7, "3E in detail")
1. Values P1 N3 B3 R5 Q9 (king none), logged only; every report carries a
   NOTE that a nonnegative material result is not proof of a good move.
2. "exchange <square> N" (N = 1..8): every legal same-square capture
   sequence, S first, sides alternating, every prefix listed (the side to
   move may stop), with WHITE-TOOK / BLACK-TOOK piece types and both raw
   totals (MATERIAL White x Black y), LIMIT on lines cut by the limit;
   NO CAPTURES when there is none (never a zero-capture "trade").
3. Per first capture: MATERIAL FORCED NONNEGATIVE / POSSIBLE NONNEGATIVE /
   ELSE in the bounded exchange model, 3C structure: the side to move
   chooses between stopping and each capture (S at S's turns, the other
   side at its turns); BOUNDED when the limit cut a line; plus the owner's
   value ORDER (greatest taken = IF).
4. "order N": the owner's ideas 7 and 8 - one combined list, rebuilt in
   every position: HEADS CAPTURE | DISTANCE; group 1 = captures MATERIAL
   FORCED (first) then POSSIBLE NONNEGATIVE, each by value taken (greatest
   = IF), ties by fewer squares; group 2 = all other moves by squares
   (fewest = IF), lines labelled GROUP 2 DISTANCE (review 27), any ELSE
   capture marked. Value, squares and class on every line; never called
   best. As no first capture is ELSE in this model, group 1 = every legal
   capture, which satisfies review 27 point 3 and review 28 point 5 at
   once; no capture is called a good trade.
5. "risk on": AT-RISK <arriving piece's value> on ROUTE lines when the goal
   square is attacked on arrival (attacked material, not a loss); off by
   default, so the version 11 report and all transcripts are unchanged.
6. Scope: same-square exchanges only (your point 6); the owner's broader
   "multiple loops of the different pieces" is recorded as later work.

A fact to note
- In this model the other side may always decline to recapture, so every
  first capture is at least MATERIAL POSSIBLE NONNEGATIVE; MATERIAL ELSE
  cannot occur for a first capture (it would need the other side to be
  forced to capture). This is stated in the spec and tested (X11). The
  meaningful split for group 1 is therefore FORCED versus POSSIBLE; group 2
  holds the non-captures. Please say whether you want a different model
  (for example "the other side must recapture when it can") in a later
  stage.
